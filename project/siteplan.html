Colors

The primary text colors are black and a specific shade of dark grey called "eigengrau" (#16161d). Black is black, but eigengrau is a shade of grey seen by the naked eye in the absence of light. That fact is not relevant to the project, it is just interesting

The main background color is darkgreen and the secondary backround color is gold

Also within the css document is four additional colors. Of these, "accent1" is used as the background color for articles. Currently, accent1 is lightskyblue. The others (in order) are crimson, midnightblue, and linen

Typography

Not counting fallbacks, the fonts being used are Goudy Bookletter 1911, Jim Nightshade, and Six Caps

Bookletter is used as the body, and Nightshade and Caps are used for headings, Nightshade is h1, and Caps is h2
